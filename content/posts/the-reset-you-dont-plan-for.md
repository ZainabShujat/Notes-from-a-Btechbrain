For the past 2–3 weeks, I kind of… disappeared.

Not in a dramatic way. Not even intentionally. Just slowly… quietly.

At first, it felt like I was just taking a break. And then at some point, it stopped feeling like a break and started feeling like I just couldn’t get myself to come back.

I was overwhelmed, overworked, and constantly in that mode of “okay just push through this and then you’ll rest.”

Except… I never got to the rest part.

And then my body just went, yeah no, we’re done here.

No focus. No motivation. No energy to even care about things I usually love doing.

And I think that’s the part no one really explains properly:

Burnout doesn’t always feel like being tired. Sometimes it just feels like you’ve disconnected from yourself.

## What I thought vs what was actually happening

I kept telling myself, “I just need to try harder.”

But the reality was… I had already pushed past the point where trying harder would help.

## So what changed?

Honestly? Nothing big.

No sudden “I’ve figured everything out” moment. No perfect routine. No glow-up reset.

Just one quiet decision:

I’m not going to try to catch up. I’m just going to start again.

- What I worked on today

Instead of forcing myself to be productive in the usual way, I just… fixed things.

Small things.

- Cleaned up my homepage so it actually makes sense
- Redesigned my post cards so they’re easier to read
- Fixed random CSS issues that were making everything look off
- Made the “Start Here” section feel like an actual starting point

Nothing huge. No big features. Just… better foundations.

And weirdly, that’s what brought me back.

## What this means going forward

This doesn’t feel like a comeback.

It feels like a reset.

A quieter, more intentional way of working.

I’m picking things back up slowly:

- continuing the sci-fi breakdown series
- exploring content creation on Instagram
- and wrapping up this phase as the last series under Notes From a B.Tech Brain

Not because I’m done.

Just because I’m growing out of what it was… into what it can become next.

## If you’ve been feeling stuck too

Maybe you don’t need a big plan right now.

Maybe you just need to:

- stop forcing it
- fix one small thing
- and let yourself come back at your own pace

I didn’t bounce back.

I just… came back.

And honestly, right now - that feels like enough. 🤍
