# 🧠 India’s AI Talent Pool: Strengths and Gaps

India’s AI workforce has crossed **400,000 professionals**, marking strong sector growth.  
However, gaps remain in:

- advanced research  
- high-end ML system design  
- specialized AI engineering  

→ **Why it matters:**  
Closing this skill gap is essential for India to solidify its position as a global AI contender.

---

# 🌾 AI-Powered Bollworm Management in Agriculture

The Union Agriculture Ministry is preparing to deploy an **AI-based system** to combat the pink bollworm threat in cotton crops.

→ **Why it matters:**  
AI-driven pest management could significantly boost crop yields and reshape how Indian agriculture handles long-standing challenges.

---

# 🛡️ National Security Gets an AI Upgrade

Union Home Minister Amit Shah unveiled an upgraded **Multi Agency Centre (MAC)** with built-in AI and Machine Learning tools for:

- improved threat detection  
- faster intelligence analysis  
- stronger inter-agency coordination  

→ **Why it matters:**  
AI is becoming a core pillar of India’s security infrastructure.

---

# 💼 Groww Acquires Fisdom in a $150M Deal

Investment platform **Groww** has agreed to acquire wealth-tech startup **Fisdom** for about **$150 million**.

→ **Why it matters:**  
This move strengthens Groww’s foothold in wealth management and signals consolidation in India’s fintech sector.

---

# 🔋 Realme GT 7 Dream Edition: A Gaming Powerhouse

Realme teased its upcoming flagship — launching **May 20** — featuring:

- a massive **7,000mAh battery**  
- **120W fast charging**  
- **120fps gaming** support  

→ **Why it matters:**  
Mobile gaming is exploding, and Realme is positioning itself directly for that audience.

---

# 💻 Dell Launches AI-Powered Plus Series in India

Dell unveiled its new **AI-powered Plus series**, including:

- Dell 14 Plus  
- Dell 14 2-in-1 Plus  
- Dell 16 Plus  

All featuring **Intel Core Ultra** processors and **Copilot+** integrations.

→ **Why it matters:**  
AI-first laptops are officially entering mainstream consumer tech.

---

# ☀️ ReNew Energy’s $2.57 Billion Renewable Project

ReNew Energy Global announced a **$2.57B investment** to build a hybrid renewable energy project in Andhra Pradesh, generating **2.8 GW** through solar + wind.

→ **Why it matters:**  
One of India’s most ambitious clean-energy projects to date — a major boost toward national sustainability goals.
