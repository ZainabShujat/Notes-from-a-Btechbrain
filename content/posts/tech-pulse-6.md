# 🔧 India’s Tech Advancements

## 1. India Launches Its First Indigenous Semiconductor Chip

India has unveiled its **first homegrown semiconductor chip**, marking a major leap in domestic hardware manufacturing.  
This move reduces reliance on imports and strengthens India’s push toward electronics self-sufficiency.

→ **Why it matters:**  
A strong semiconductor ecosystem is essential for national security, AI innovation, and stable tech supply chains.

---

## 2. East Central Railway Introduces AI-Based Safety System

The East Central Railway (ECR) has deployed an **AI-driven safety and maintenance system** that:

- automates coach cleaning  
- manages water refilling  
- detects fire hazards  
- flags brake issues  
- identifies equipment failures  

→ **Why it matters:**  
AI is becoming a critical tool for railway modernization, improving both passenger safety and operational efficiency.

---

## 3. Kerala Forest Department Uses Drones for Wildlife Monitoring

To reduce human–wildlife conflict, the Kerala Forest Department plans to use **drones** to monitor animal movement in high-risk zones.

→ **Why it matters:**  
Tech-enabled conservation ensures safer interactions for both communities and wildlife.

## 2. Microsoft Showcases “Agentic AI” at Build 2025

Microsoft highlighted its new concept of **agentic AI** — systems capable of performing complex tasks autonomously with higher reasoning and planning.

→ **Why it matters:**  
This represents a major step toward intelligent, self-directed AI applications.

---

## 3. India Approves Indigenous Stealth Fighter Jet Development

India’s Defence Ministry has approved development of a **fifth-generation, twin-engine stealth fighter jet** to modernize the Indian Air Force and reduce foreign dependence.

→ **Why it matters:**  
A strategic move amid rising regional security challenges.
