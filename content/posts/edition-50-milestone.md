Hi there.  
We made it.

Exactly 50 editions ago, I sat with an overload of thoughts and a blank Sketchbook canvas, doodling a little “Loading…” screen, a lightbulb, and a tiny pink brain. I didn’t know what this newsletter would become. I didn’t even know if I’d keep going.

But something changed when I pressed “Post.”

Something clicked.

![article content](/assets/articles/milestone50/pic1.webp)  
*The digitally drawn logo*

And now here we are: Edition Fifty.  
A number that looks simple but carries seven months of growth, chaos, rebuilding, tiny victories, and big shifts.

## The Beginning: A Girl, a Brain, and a Lot of Confusion

When I launched Edition 1 (*A Beginning Amidst the Chaos*), I wrote because I felt directionless. Everyone around me looked like they were fitting in, joining clubs, participating, thriving.

Meanwhile, I was:

- trying to learn Python, C, Java  
- rediscovering digital art  
- feeling lost  
- and craving a place to put all the thoughts that wouldn’t fit inside me anymore

So I made one.

![article content](/assets/articles/milestone50/pic2.webp)

A small weekly corner where:

- tech made sense  
- soft skills felt human  
- news didn’t feel overwhelming  
- and girlhood had a voice  

Somewhere between Edition 1 and Edition 50, I went from:  
**trying to fit in → to building my own space.**

## The Arc No One Warns You About

Between April and now, I learned something I wasn’t expecting:

**You don’t fit in by gossiping.  
You don’t fit in by shrinking.  
You fit in by being EXACTLY the nerd you are.**

And that’s been the voice of *Notes From a B. Tech Brain*:

- curious  
- observant  
- emotional  
- a little chaotic  
- always honest  

This newsletter didn’t just become a routine.  
It became a place where I grew — as a student, as a woman, as a developer, as a writer.

## The Projects Built Alongside the Pages

These 50 editions weren’t written alone.  
Along the way, I built a life around them:

🧠 **MERN Blog — Notes From a B. Tech Brain Website**  
A full-stack home for my writing. (although still in the making)

💝 **SibsLove**

🌐 **HTML/CSS Blog Prototype**

These weren’t “extra achievements.”  
They were footprints on the road to 50.

- the beginning of Volume II  

I didn’t rush it. I didn’t fake it.  
I built toward it — post by post, thought by thought, edition by edition.

## What 50 Taught Me

These aren’t lessons. These are realizations:

- Consistency isn’t discipline. It’s *love*.  
- Nerdiness is not something to hide — it’s something to **lead with**.  
- Writing is thinking.  
- Coding is storytelling.  
- Growth looks quiet, not glamorous.  
- You can rebuild yourself in public without breaking.  
- Your 19–20-year-old brain deserves to be remembered.  

And most importantly:

**Sometimes, you don’t find your place.  
You create it.**
