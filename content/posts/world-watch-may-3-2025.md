# India–Pakistan Tensions Rise After Pahalgam Attack

Following the tragic attack in Pahalgam that took 26 lives, diplomatic relations between India and Pakistan have taken a hit.  
Flight restrictions, cancelled trade routes, suspended visas, and even water-sharing talks under the Indus Waters Treaty have been paused.

It’s an intense moment geopolitically.

**Why it matters:**  
This goes beyond borders. It affects national security, diplomacy, and even water rights — something we can’t afford to ignore.

---

# ☀️ Extreme Heatwaves in South Asia

With temperatures crossing **48°C** in parts of Pakistan and rising steadily across Indian states like Rajasthan, Bihar, and UP, South Asia is under major heat stress.

Health systems are feeling the pressure, and there’s concern around crop yields and energy demand.

**Why it matters:**  
This is a direct glimpse into climate change on the ground.  
It's no longer a future problem — it’s already here.

---

# Trump Eyes Trade Moves with India

Donald Trump has hinted at forming “new deals” with India, South Korea, and Japan.  
Whether or not he returns to office, these statements already spark market conversations and trade speculation.

**Why it matters:**  
India’s exports and global partnerships could be reshaped depending on what unfolds.  
Watching closely is essential.

---

# 🎬 India Hosts First Global Entertainment Summit

Mumbai is currently hosting **WAVES 2024** — the first World Audio Visual and Entertainment Summit.  
It brings together leaders from tech, media, film, and gaming to explore India’s creative industry potential.

**Why it matters:**  
It’s a sign that India’s creative economy is growing into a global force — from film and animation to gaming and digital innovation.

---

# 📉 IMF Lowers India’s Growth Forecast

The IMF has revised India’s growth outlook from **6.5%** to **6.2%**, citing global economic volatility, youth unemployment, and the urgency of structural reforms in land and labour.

**Why it matters:**  
While India still leads global growth charts, this drop reminds us that internal stability and reform are key to long-term momentum.
