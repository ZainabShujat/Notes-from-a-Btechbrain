# 1. Closed Airspace & Water Wars ⚠️

Pakistan’s airspace remains **closed to Indian flights**, disrupting key international routes.  
Meanwhile, India’s decision to suspend the **Indus Waters Treaty** triggered aggressive responses from Pakistan, with threats of escalation and growing global concern over water security.

# 2. Monsoon Mayhem: Too Much & Too Little 💧

India saw a **rainfall surplus in June**, but certain regions like **Marathwada** face a **40% deficit**, risking crop failures and a potential kharif season collapse.  
The larger picture: climate change is turning monsoons from predictable lifelines into unpredictable threats.

🔗 Source: https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/land-use-biodiversity/india-poised-above-average-july-rains-after-surplus-june-2025-06-30

---

# 3. Trade Tensions Rise: India vs. US Tariffs 🏛️

India is preparing **WTO retaliation** against U.S. auto tariffs impacting **$2.89 bn** in exports.  
Meanwhile, former U.S. President Trump warned of a **200% tariff** on pharma products — a worrying prospect for India’s pharmaceutical exporters.

🔗 Source: https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/donald-trump-new-tariffs-us-imposes-50-pc-tariffs-on-copper-warns-of-200-pc-duty-on-pharma-how-will-it-impact-india-8846183

# 4. Mass Strikes Shake India ⚒️

On **July 9**, a **Bharat Bandh** saw nationwide participation by labour unions, farmers, and transport workers.  
The protests raised alarms over worker rights, labour code changes, and frustrations around privatisation.

🔗 Source: https://m.economictimes.com/news/new-updates/school-assembly-news-headlines-10-july-2025-top-national-international-sports-and-business-updates/articleshow/122344940.cms

# 5. Israel–Iran Escalation (June 13) 🚨

Israel launched **Operation Rising Lion**, conducting airstrikes targeting Iran’s nuclear and military sites. Iran responded with **missiles and drones**, sharply raising regional tensions.

🔗 Source: (Reuters — Israel–Iran missiles clash)

# 6. Gaza Ceasefire Talks Resume (July 7–10) 🕊️

Indirect **Qatar-mediated talks** resumed between Gaza and Israel, discussing:

- A potential **60-day truce**  
- Phased hostage release  
- Creation of buffer zones and humanitarian corridors

🔗 Source: (Reuters — Gaza ceasefire talks)
